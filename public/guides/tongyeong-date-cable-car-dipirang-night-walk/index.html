Tongyeong Date: Cable Car Views, Harbor Sunset & DIPIRANG Night Light Walk

Oct 29, 20262 min read
Prices, opening hours and transit times change often. We last checked them on Oct 29, 2026. Please confirm on the official site or Naver Map before you go.

Tongyeong, on Korea's south coast, is lovely by day and even better after dark. Take the cable car up for island views, watch the sun set over the harbor, then walk a forest trail lit with media art.

Quick facts

  • From Seoul: express bus about 4 hours; from Busan about 1h 40m
  • Sunset: about 17:25 on Nov 10, 17:17 on Nov 25 (calculated)
  • Avoid Wednesdays: DIPIRANG is closed every Wednesday; the cable car closes the 2nd and 4th Wednesdays

1. Tongyeong Cable Car

Nearly 2 km up Mireuksan, with views over the islands of the Hallyeosu Waterway.

  • Address: 205 Balgae-ro, Tongyeong (Korean: 경남 통영시 발개로 205)
  • Hours: weekdays 10:00–18:00, weekends 09:00–18:00; may close early
  • Fares: adults 17,000 won round trip, children 13,000 won · free parking
  • It stops in strong wind, so check before you go.

2. Dongpirang Mural Village & Jungang Market

Dongpirang's hillside alleys are covered in murals; a loop takes about 20 minutes. People live here, so keep your voice down and don't photograph into homes. Below, at Jungang Market, you can choose live fish and have it served at a nearby restaurant for a table fee. The market closes early, so go before 7 PM. Try Tongyeong's famous honey bread (kkulppang).

3. Sunset at Gangguan Harbor

Walk down from Dongpirang to Gangguan Harbor just in time for sunset over the moored boats.

4. DIPIRANG: a night trail of light

A 1.3 km forest trail in Nammangsan Park with 15 themed light and media-art zones.

  • Address: 29 Nammanggongwon-gil, Tongyeong (Korean: 경남 통영시 남망공원길 29)
  • Winter hours (Oct–Feb): 18:30–22:00, last entry 21:00
  • Tickets: adults 15,000 won, teens 12,000 won, children 10,000 won · free parking
  • Closed Wednesdays, January 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok

Route at a glance

  1. 14:00 Cable car
  2. 15:30 Dongpirang
  3. 16:30 Jungang Market and honey bread
  4. 17:15 Gangguan sunset
  5. 18:00 Dinner
  6. 19:30 DIPIRANG night walk

Tips for foreign travelers

  • Next morning, try chungmu gimbap: plain rice rolls with spicy squid and radish on the side.
  • Wear flat shoes for the dark trail and bring a scarf for the sea wind.
  • Stay near Gangguan to walk back after DIPIRANG.

Summary

Sea views from the cable car, a harbor sunset and a glowing night trail: Tongyeong gives you day, dusk and night in one date. Just avoid Wednesdays.
