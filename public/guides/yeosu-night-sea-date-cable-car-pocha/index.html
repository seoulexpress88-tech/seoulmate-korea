Yeosu Night Sea Date: Sunset Cable Car, Night Cruise & Romantic Pocha Street

Nov 1, 20262 min read

Koreans all know the song "Yeosu Night Sea." In November the city is quieter than in summer, which makes it even more romantic, and with early sunsets you can catch both the sunset and the night lights in one evening if you time it right.

Quick facts

  • From Seoul: KTX to Yeosu-EXPO Station, about 3 hours; stay one night
  • Sunset (calculated): Nov 15 at 17:26, Nov 20 at 17:23, Nov 25 at 17:21
  • Be in place by about 16:50: the sky is prettiest in the 30 minutes before sunset

1. Afternoon: Gosodong Angel Mural Village

After lunch, walk the mural alleys that run from Jinnamgwan Hall toward the waterfront park. The route is 1,004 m long, and since 1004 sounds like "cheonsa" (angel) in Korean, it is called the Angel Mural Village. Along the way you look down on Dolsan Bridge and Geobukseon Bridge, with plenty of murals for couple photos. The park at the end is a good night-view spot too.

2. Sea-view cafe break

  • Cafe Hayan Pado (카페 하얀파도), Dolsan: view of Dolsan Bridge, 10:30–21:00
  • Dal, Coffee (달,커피), Seomdalcheon: rooftop cafe known for sunsets, weekdays 09:30–19:00, weekends until 20:00
  • Hours and booking

3. Sunset: the sea cable car's Crystal cabin

Watch the sunset from above the water. The cabins are private for two, and the glass-floored Crystal cabin is the popular date pick.

  • Round trip (adults): standard 17,000 won, Crystal 24,000 won
  • Hours: 09:30–21:30 (tickets until 21:00)
  • Tip: lines get long at sunset; arrive around 16:30

4. Night: Dolsan Park view or a night cruise

Dolsan Park, near the cable car's Dolsan station, is known for the bridge lights reflecting on the sea. Lighting hours. For the view from the water, take a night cruise:

  • Minam Cruise: night-view tour adults 25,000 won, fireworks cruise 45,000 won
  • Isabu Cruise: adults 35,000 won on site; fireworks cruises Fri–Sun and holidays
  • Departure times change monthly with sunset

5. Finish at Nangman Pocha street

End the night at Nangman Pocha, a row of seaside tent bars under Geobukseon Bridge, where you eat seafood dishes with a drink by the water.

  • Nov–Feb hours: 17:00–24:00
  • Closed: first Wednesday of each month · cards accepted · parking available
  • Currently operating

The sea wind is cold, so bring a warm jacket.

Route at a glance

  1. 13:00 Angel Mural Village
  2. 15:00 Sea-view cafe
  3. 16:30 Sea cable car → sunset
  4. 18:00 Dolsan Park night view or a night cruise
  5. 20:00 Nangman Pocha

Stay in Dolsan or near Yeosu-EXPO Station, and add a morning walk on Odongdo the next day.

Tips for foreign travelers

  • Taxis are the easiest way between Dolsan and the old town at night; show the Korean name, such as 낭만포차 or 돌산공원.
  • Use Naver Map or KakaoMap for directions.
  • Pocha prices are usually per dish to share, not per person.

Summary

Mural alleys by day, a cafe, the cable car at sunset, night lights and Nangman Pocha. Take the KTX and give Yeosu a relaxed two days.
