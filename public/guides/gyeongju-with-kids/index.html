Gyeongju with Kids in November: 5 Family-Friendly Spots (Stroller Tips & Rainy-Day Options)

Oct 27, 20263 min read
Prices, opening hours and transit times change often. We last checked them on Oct 27, 2026. Please confirm on the official site or Naver Map before you go.

Traveling Korea with little ones? Gyeongju is one of the easiest cities to do with kids — flat paths, giant grassy tombs that look like hills, and a world-class museum that's completely free.

November is a great time to go: foliage in the region is forecast to peak in early to mid-November 2026. Here are 5 spots picked with parents in mind — stroller access, restrooms, nursing rooms, and what to do if it rains.

1. Daereungwon & Cheomseongdae — an easy stroller walk

Start here. The paths are flat and wide, so pushing a stroller is easy. Kids usually ask, "Are those hills?" — they're actually the tombs of Silla kings, which tends to get a big reaction.

  • Admission: Daereungwon and Cheomseongdae are free. Inside Cheonmachong tomb: adults ₩3,000, children ₩1,000
  • Hours: Daereungwon 09:00–22:00 / Cheomseongdae until 21:00 from November
  • Best for: toddlers and preschoolers
  • Tip: there's little shade or seating, so bring water and snacks

2. Gyeongju National Museum + Children's Museum — free and excellent

If you only pick one place with kids, make it this one. General admission is free, and the Children's Museum is free too — but you need an online reservation. Each session is limited to 60 people, so book ahead for weekends.

  • Hours: 10:00–18:00 / Children's Museum runs 8 sessions a day
  • Closure: the Children's Museum is temporarily closed on Monday, November 2
  • Facilities: nursing room (with water dispenser and sink) and stroller parking
  • Parking: free for the first 20 minutes, then paid
  • Booking: gyeongju.museum.go.kr / +82-54-740-7591

It's indoors, so it's also your best plan B for rain or cold.

3. Tongiljeon Ginkgo Road — perfect for nap time

Kid fell asleep in the car? Drive slowly down this road. Ginkgo trees line both sides, so it feels like passing through a golden tunnel.

If they're awake, hop out and let them throw armfuls of leaves. Kids love it.

  • Location: entrance road to Tongiljeon, east of Namsan
  • Heads up: fallen ginkgo nuts smell. Use shoes you don't mind getting dirty.

4. Ungok Seowon — run around under a 370-year-old tree

One enormous ginkgo, said to be over 370 years old, turns the whole courtyard yellow. It's far quieter than downtown, so kids can run around freely.

  • Address: 79-13 Saryang-gil, Gangdong-myeon, Gyeongju (경북 경주시 강동면 사랴길 79-13)
  • Admission & parking: free (about 80 spaces)
  • Peak: early November
  • Note: hard to reach by bus — take a taxi or drive

5. Gyeongju Donggungwon — indoor botanical garden & bird park

A good backup when the weather is unsure. There's a botanical garden and a separate bird park.

  • Botanical garden: adults ₩5,000, elementary students ₩3,000, preschoolers free
  • Bird park (separate ticket): adults ₩20,000, 24 months–elementary ₩15,000, under 24 months free
  • Hours: 09:30–19:00 (last ticket 18:00)
  • Parking: free, 219 spaces

The botanical garden ticket does not include the bird park — don't get confused at the ticket office.

An easy one-day route for families

With kids, three places a day is plenty.

  • Morning: Gyeongju National Museum + Children's Museum (match your booked session)
  • Lunch: something light — gimbap or noodles
  • Nap time: drive the Tongiljeon ginkgo road
  • Afternoon: Daereungwon and Cheomseongdae walk
  • Staying overnight? Visit Ungok Seowon the next morning.

Kid-friendly food

Gyori Gimbap (main branch) — mild egg-filled gimbap that most kids happily eat. Opens early, so grab some before you head out.

  • 2 Tapri 3-gil, Gyeongju (경북 경주시 탑리3길 2)
  • 2 rolls ₩11,000 / noodles ₩6,500, closed Wednesdays

Tips for families visiting from abroad

  • Big temperature swings: pack a light down jacket for the kids.
  • Rain knocks the leaves down fast — check the forecast before you go.
  • Weekend parking near Daereungwon and Hwangnidan-gil is tough. Go early.
  • Car seats: taxis usually don't have them. If you need one, consider a rental car that offers child seats.
  • Maps: use Naver Map or KakaoMap instead of Google Maps for accurate directions.

Summary

Stroller walk: Daereungwon. Rainy day: the museum and Donggungwon. Photos: Ungok Seowon and Tongiljeon.
