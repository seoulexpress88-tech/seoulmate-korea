Festivals & seasons

  • May or June (lunar calendar)Gangneung Danoje Festival 강릉단오제

    A UNESCO-listed traditional festival with rituals, mask plays and a huge market.

  • OctoberGangneung Coffee Festival 강릉커피축제

    Roasters and cafés from across the country.

  • January 1New Year sunrise 해맞이

    Crowds gather at Gyeongpo and Jeongdongjin for the first sunrise.

  • About December to MarchSki season in Pyeongchang 평창 스키 시즌

    Yongpyong and Phoenix, the 2018 Olympic resorts, are about 40 minutes away. Opening dates are announced in November.

Dates change every year. Confirm on the official site before you book.
