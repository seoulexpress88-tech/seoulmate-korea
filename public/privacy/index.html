Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

This site doesn't require an account. The trip planner runs entirely in your browser; the dates, cities and choices you enter are not sent to us.

What is collected

  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Links to Google Maps, Naver Map and booking partners take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies, analytics and ads

We don't set cookies today. If we add analytics, advertising (such as Google AdSense) or affiliate tracking, we'll update this page and, where required, ask for your consent first.
