Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

This site doesn't require an account. The trip planner runs entirely in your browser; the dates, cities and choices you enter are not sent to us.

What is collected

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Vercel, records standard request data (IP address, browser, page requested, time) to run and secure the site, under its own retention rules.
  • Ads. We show ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies (see below).
  • Fonts. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we keep your message and address to reply, for up to one year after our last reply.
  • We don't collect your name, payment details or device location, and we don't sell your information.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can opt out of some other vendors' cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices (or youronlinechoices.eu in Europe).
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • If you refuse personalized ads, you may still see ads that aren't based on your past browsing.
  • We don't show ads in the trip planner.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message from Google (a Google-certified consent management platform) asks for your choice before ad cookies are used.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies won't stop the site from working.

Affiliate links

Links labeled "affiliate link" go to partner companies. When you click one, the partner may use cookies on its own site to credit us for a booking. Partners don't send us your name or contact details. See our affiliate disclosure.

Who receives information

We don't sell or rent your personal information. Vercel Inc. (hosting, server logs) and Google LLC (ads, consent, fonts) process information in the United States under their own terms.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. In the EEA or the UK we rely on your consent for ad cookies and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the site for server logs; you can withdraw consent at any time and complain to your local data protection authority. In Korea you can also contact the Personal Information Protection Commission's Privacy Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, call 118).

Children

The site is for adults planning trips. We don't knowingly collect information from children.
