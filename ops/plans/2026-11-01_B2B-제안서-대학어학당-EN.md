> **한국어 요약 (3줄)**
> 1. 어학당·국제처에 **일정 설계(Program Design)와 오리엔테이션용 영문 가이드북**을 우리가 직접 팔고, 차량·가이드·입장 등 현지 운영은 **등록 종합여행사가 직접 계약·청구**하는 제안서다. 우리가 투어를 운영하거나 예약을 대행한다고 읽히는 문장은 넣지 않았다.
> 2. 가격은 상품 구조 v2 기준: 5일·30명 프로그램 설계 **₩150만부터**, 1~2일 현장학습 설계·가이드북·현지 운영은 **견적**. 가짜 후기·실적·고객사 없음, "아직 기관 실적이 없다"를 FAQ에 그대로 적었다.
> 3. 국내 기관에 이메일로 보내면 정보통신망법 제50조(사전 동의) 문제가 있다. 그래서 **발송 경로는 규리가 정해야 한다**(공식 문의 폼 절차 문의 → 상대가 요청하면 제안서 전달 방식 권장).
>
> 목표 기여: 단체 1건이 개인 상품 수십 건과 같다. 설계비는 우리 매출(1건 ₩150만+), 현지 운영은 제휴 수수료 12%(v2 P3)로 1억 목표의 B2B 몫을 맡는다. 미션의 "직접 즐길 수 있게"를 유학생 단체로 넓힌다.

# Seoulmate for Korean Language Programs
### Winter field-trip design and an English orientation guidebook for your students

Prepared for: [Institution name] · [Date] · Draft for discussion

## 1. The problem we want to help with

Winter programs (December to February) are short, and students arrive wanting to see Korea, not just the classroom. For program staff, each field trip means:

- Planning days that work for a mixed group: different countries, languages, budgets, dietary and religious needs, and sometimes mobility needs.
- Making English materials students will actually read: where to meet, how to get there, what to wear, what to do if they get lost.
- Having a plan B for cold or snowy days.
- Answering the same first-week questions again and again: transport cards, SIM/eSIM, getting around the city.

This takes time your team could spend on teaching and student care.

## 2. What we propose

Seoulmate is an English-language Korea travel platform with a free trip planner and travel guides ([seoulmate-korea-iota.vercel.app](https://seoulmate-korea-iota.vercel.app)). For language programs we offer two information services, plus a clear route to on-the-ground services.

### A. Program Design (from Seoulmate)
A ready-to-use field-trip plan for your cohort.
- Day-by-day itinerary with timings, walking distances and transport between stops
- Options by budget and by student needs (dietary, prayer times, accessibility)
- An indoor plan B for each outdoor day
- Booking information for each venue: official links and how to book (your team or the travel agency books)
- An estimated budget sheet for your planning (estimates only; final prices come from venues and the agency)
- Two rounds of revisions

### B. Orientation Guidebook (from Seoulmate)
An English PDF you can hand out or use in your orientation session.
- First-week basics: transport cards, getting around, phone/SIM options, useful apps
- Field-trip pages: meeting points, what to bring, winter clothing tips, simple Korean phrases
- Your program's own contact and emergency information on the front page (provided by you)
- Can be co-branded with your program name

### C. On-the-ground services (from a registered Korean travel agency, not from Seoulmate)
If your group needs a chartered bus, guiding, entrance arrangements or meals, these are **contracted, operated and invoiced directly by a registered Korean general travel agency**. The agency is your contracting party for those services and provides its own contract, terms and safety information.

To be clear about roles: Seoulmate designs itineraries and provides travel information. We do not operate tours, make bookings or collect payment for ground services. We are finalising that agency partnership now and will name the agency before any quote.

## 5. FAQ

**Are you a travel agency?**
No. Seoulmate designs itineraries and provides travel information. Transport, guiding and other on-the-ground services are provided by a registered Korean general travel agency under its own contract.

**Who is responsible during the trip?**
For services contracted with the agency, the agency is responsible under its contract, including safety information and insurance it is required to provide. Your program staff remain responsible for supervising students as usual. Seoulmate is responsible for the accuracy of the information in our materials.

**Will tour guides be licensed?**
Under Korean law, guiding foreign tourists for a travel agency requires a licensed Tourist Interpretation Guide (관광통역안내사). Guided services would be arranged by the agency under that rule. [Compliance check before sending]

**Can you book the venues for us?**
We give you the official booking link and step-by-step instructions for every venue. If you want someone to make the bookings, the agency can do this as part of its services.

**Do you need students' personal data?**
No. For design we only need group-level information (numbers, nationalities if relevant, dietary and access needs as counts). If the agency needs participant details for its services, it will request them directly under its own privacy terms.

**How do we pay?**
Design and guidebook: Seoulmate invoice [payment method and tax invoice details: CHECK]. Ground services: paid directly to the agency.

**What if our dates or numbers change?**
Design work includes two revision rounds. Changes to ground services follow the agency's terms.

**Do you have references from other institutions?**
Not yet. Seoulmate launched in 2026 and this is a new service for language programs. That is why we offer a sample itinerary first, so you can judge the quality directly.

**Which languages?**
Materials are in English. [Other languages: not offered yet]
