Looking for a romantic evening in Seoul that doesn't cost much? This route links a palace that stays open until 9 PM, a historic walking street, a little-known free observation deck and Namsan at sunset.

For the best daytime foliage spots, see our Seoul autumn foliage guide. This one is all about the evening.

Quick facts

  • Seoul sunset: Nov 15 about 17:22, Nov 20 about 17:19, Nov 25 about 17:16
  • Best days: Friday or Saturday, when the free observatory is open at night
  • Avoid Mondays: Deoksugung is closed

1. Deoksugung Palace (1,000 won)

  • Tickets: adults 1,000 won; free if you wear hanbok
  • Hours: 09:00–21:00 (last entry 20:00); closed Mondays
  • Address: 99 Sejong-daero, Jung-gu (Korean: 서울 중구 세종대로 99) · City Hall Station, Lines 1 and 2
  • Seokjojeon Hall's Korean Empire history museum runs guided visits by online reservation

2. Jeongdong-gil walk & a free art museum

Follow the palace's stone wall into Jeongdong-gil, lined with 19th-century churches, schools and theaters. On the way, the Seoul Museum of Art (Seosomun main building) has a free exhibition this season.

3. Jeongdong Observatory: a free view over the palace

On the 13th floor of Seoul City Hall's Seosomun building, this observation deck looks straight down on Deoksugung. It's free with no reservation, and since September 2026 it opens late on Fridays and Saturdays.

  • Mon–Thu: 13:30–17:30 · Fri: 13:30–22:00 · Sat: 09:00–22:00
  • November–February: night opening ends at 21:00 · closed on public holidays
  • There's a café inside, so grab a warm drink with the view.

4. Namsan at sunset

  • Namsan cable car: 10:00–23:00; round trip adults 15,000 won
  • N Seoul Tower observatory: adults 29,000 won
  • Cable car lines can take 1–2 hours. The Namsan circular buses 01A and 01B from Myeongdong are much faster.
  • To catch sunset, be on Namsan by about 4:45 PM. You can also reverse the order: Namsan sunset first, then the observatory's night view.

5. Cafés and wine below Namsan

Huam-dong and Haebangchon, on the slopes below Namsan, are full of cafés in converted old houses and small wine bars. Weekend evenings fill up, so check opening hours and reservations.

Route at a glance

  1. 14:00 Deoksugung
  2. 15:30 Jeongdong-gil and the art museum
  3. 16:45 Namsan by circular bus, sunset
  4. 18:30 Dinner in Huam-dong or Haebangchon
  5. 20:00 Friday or Saturday: Jeongdong Observatory night view (until 21:00)

Tips for foreign travelers

  • Deoksugung is open in the evening on weekdays too, an easy after-work date.
  • Use Naver Map for the Namsan circular bus stops; tap your T-money card when boarding.
  • Bring gloves: Namsan's summit is windy after dark.

Summary

A 1,000-won palace, a walk along Jeongdong-gil, Namsan at sunset and a free palace view from above. Romantic, central and easy on the wallet.
