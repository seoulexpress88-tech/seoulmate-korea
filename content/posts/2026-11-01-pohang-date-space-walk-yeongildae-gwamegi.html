Pohang, on Korea's east coast, packs more into a date than you'd expect: a walkable sculpture shaped like a roller coaster, a pavilion standing in the sea that glows at night, and in November and December, gwamegi season. Here is a weekend day from a gwamegi lunch to a late-night observatory, and almost everything is free.

Quick facts

  • From Seoul: KTX from Seoul Station to Pohang, about 2.5 hours
  • Sunset: in the 5 PM hour in mid-November
  • No cable car: the once-planned Yeongil Bay sea cable car was cancelled, so don't go looking for it

1. Guryongpo gwamegi: free tastings on weekends

Gwamegi is half-dried Pacific saury (or herring), a winter delicacy wrapped in seaweed with green onion and garlic. On November and December weekend afternoons, the Gwamegi Culture Center runs free tastings, a low-risk way to try it first.

  • Where: 3rd floor, Guryongpo Gwamegi Culture Center, Pohang
  • When: every Saturday and Sunday in Nov–Dec, 14:00–17:00
  • Price: free (may end early if it runs out)
  • Restaurants:

2. Space Walk: time it for sunset

A walkable steel sculpture on a hill in Hwanho Park that looks like a roller coaster. You climb real stairs with Yeongil Bay below, and at night LED lights make it glow. In winter it closes early, at 18:00 on weekends, which lines up almost exactly with sunset. Go up around 16:30 to catch both the sunset and the lights.

  • Address: 30 Hwanhogongwon-gil, Buk-gu, Pohang (Korean: 경북 포항시 북구 환호공원길 30)
  • Hours (Nov–Mar): weekdays 10:00–17:00, weekends 10:00–18:00
  • Closed: first Monday of each month (the next day if a holiday)
  • Free · free parking at Hwanho Park
  • Minimum height 110 cm; closes automatically in rain or strong wind

3. Yeongildae sea pavilion at night

A traditional-style pavilion built out over the water that becomes a night-view spot when lit. It is close to Hwanho Park, and the beach promenade is lined with places for dinner.

  • Address: around 685-1 Duho-dong, Buk-gu, Pohang (Korean: 경북 포항시 북구 두호동 685-1 일대)
  • Open: always
  • Parking: 500 won for the first 20 minutes, then 200 won per 10 minutes (card only)
  • Dinner and cafe picks

4. Haeoreum Bridge observatory, open until 10 PM

A free observatory that started night opening in September 2026: a perfect last stop. Last entry is 21:45, so take your time over dinner.

  • Where: Songdo-dong, Nam-gu, Pohang
  • Hours: 09:00–22:00 (last entry 21:45) · free, no booking
  • Holds about 50 people at a time, so expect a short wait on weekend nights
  • No toilets inside; use those at Yeongildae or Songdo Beach

Route at a glance (weekend)

  1. 12:00 Gwamegi lunch in Guryongpo
  2. 14:00 Free tasting at the Gwamegi Culture Center
  3. 16:00 Space Walk for sunset and lights (travel time from Guryongpo)
  4. 18:00 Yeongildae night view and dinner
  5. 20:30 Haeoreum Bridge observatory

On a weekday there is no tasting, so eat at a restaurant and reach the Space Walk before its 17:00 close.

Tips for foreign travelers

  • Guryongpo is a fair distance from the city; a taxi or rental car (with an International Driving Permit) is easiest. Use KakaoMap or Naver Map.
  • Wear gloves and non-slip flat shoes for the Space Walk stairs, plus a windproof jacket.
  • Stay near Yeongildae Beach to walk to the night views.

Summary

Start with gwamegi, then sunset on the Space Walk, the glowing pavilion and a late-night observatory. A full, mostly free Pohang date.
