Does your child love cartoons? Then Chuncheon, a lake city east of Seoul, is worth a day. The Animation Museum and Toy Robot Hall sit side by side, and with a glass skywalk over the river and dakgalbi for lunch you have a full day. Closing days differ by place, so we flag those too.

Quick facts

  • From Seoul: ITX-Cheongchun train from Yongsan or Cheongnyangni to Chuncheon Station, about 1–1.5 hours, or by car
  • Best days: Wednesday to Sunday (museum closed Mondays, skywalk closed Tuesdays)
  • Best for: toddlers to upper primary; great for siblings of different ages

1. Animation Museum & Toy Robot Hall

Cartoon history plus robots, made for kids. Little ones enjoy the characters, older kids the robot activities. It is indoors, so it is a solid plan for rain or wind.

  • Address: 854 Baksa-ro, Seo-myeon, Chuncheon (Korean: 강원특별자치도 춘천시 서면 박사로 854)
  • Hours: 10:00–18:00 (last entry 17:00)
  • Closed: Mondays (the next day if a holiday), January 1, Lunar New Year and Chuseok days
  • Tickets: 7,000 won (Chuncheon residents 5,000 won), under 24 months free · child fares
  • Parking and nursing room:

2. Soyang River Skywalk: your ticket comes back as a voucher

A long glass-floored walkway stretching over the river. The fun part: you pay 2,000 won and get a Chuncheon Love gift voucher to spend at nearby shops, so it feels almost free. Whether it is the full amount. It is listed as barrier-free with ramps and a wheelchair lift, so strollers should be mostly fine.

  • Address: 2663 Yeongseo-ro, Chuncheon (Korean: 강원특별자치도 춘천시 영서로 2663)
  • Winter hours: 10:00–17:30 or 18:00
  • Closed: Tuesdays and in bad weather
  • Ticket: 2,000 won (voucher included) · paid public parking

3. Lunch: Chuncheon dakgalbi

Chuncheon is the home of dakgalbi, spicy stir-fried chicken cooked at your table. Worried about spice? Some places do a mild soy-sauce version or a kids' menu. Add a bowl of makguksu (buckwheat noodles), which kids usually like. Go around 11:30 on weekends to beat the wait.

What about LEGOLAND?

Many people think of LEGOLAND Korea when they hear Chuncheon. In 2025 it ran a winter festival, "Merry Bricksmas," from November 21, but in some past years it closed for months in winter. Check this year's November–December schedule on the official site.

A one-day plan

  1. 10:00 Animation Museum & Toy Robot Hall (about 2 hours)
  2. 12:30 Dakgalbi lunch
  3. 14:00 Soyang River Skywalk (30–60 minutes)
  4. 15:00 Snacks with your voucher → head home

Tips for foreign travelers

  • From Chuncheon Station, taxis are the quickest way to the museum; show the Korean name 애니메이션박물관.
  • Dakgalbi sauce stains: bring a spare top or ask for an apron.
  • The riverside is windy; visit the skywalk while the sun is out.

Summary

Indoor museum, dakgalbi, then a walk over the river: a Chuncheon day that ends just as kids run out of energy.
